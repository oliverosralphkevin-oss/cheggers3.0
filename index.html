Checkers Rules

  • 1. Standard Movement: Regular pieces move diagonally forward one dark square at a time.
  • 2. Capturing & Multi-Jumps: Jump diagonally (forward or backward) over an adjacent opponent piece into an empty square to capture it. If another jump is available immediately from your new spot, you continue jumping!
  • 3. Flying Kings: Reaching the far edge crowns your piece as a King, allowing long-range diagonal movement and captures across multiple empty squares.
  • 4. Winning the Match: Capture all of your opponent's pieces before the turn timer runs out to claim victory!
